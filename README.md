# SG Property Eligibility

A single-page dashboard answering who can buy or sell an HDB flat, EC or private home in Singapore.

- **Eligibility checker** — rule-by-rule verdict for buying (BTO, resale, EC, private) or selling, with grants.
- **Loan & upfront costs** — maximum bank/HDB loan (LTV, TDSR, MSR), monthly instalment, down payment, stamp duties, fees and property tax.
- **Questions & answers**, **rule tables** and a **timeline of changes** since 2016.

Rules were checked on 17 Sep 2026 against official HDB, CPF Board, URA, IRAS, MAS and MOF pages, linked throughout. It is a guide only; HDB's HFE letter and your bank's approval are the official decisions.

Open `index.html` in a browser — everything is in that one file.
