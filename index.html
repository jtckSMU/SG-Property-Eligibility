          SSD is charged on the higher of the selling price or market value, and must be paid within 14 days of the sale contract. The holding period runs from the date you accepted the Option to Purchase (or signed the S&P Agreement, or the Agreement for Lease for a new HDB flat). Mortgage duty: 0.4% of the loan, capped at $500. ABSD is fully waived on HDB flats and new ECs when any buyer is a citizen (PR-only HDB buyers pay 5%).           Key rule changes for buying and selling homes in Singapore since 2016, newest first. Each entry links to the official announcement or rule page.

          1. 2027
          2. Feb 2027

            Extra ballot chances for children

            First-timer families with or expecting children get one more ballot chance for each Singapore Citizen child aged 18 or under, in all BTO and SBF applications. HDB

          3. 2026
          4. Nov 2026

            Longer Community Care Apartment leases

            Lease choices of 15–45 years, in 5-year steps. Before this exercise, the range was 15–35 years. HDB

          5. 25 Sep 2026

            HFE documents due for the November BTO

            HDB asks buyers to apply for an HFE letter and submit all documents by this date. The November 2026 exercise offers about 7,960 flats in Bedok, Geylang, Sembawang, Tengah, Toa Payoh and Yishun. HDB

          6. 24 Aug 2026

            Income ceilings raised

            Families $14,000 → $16,000 and singles $7,000 → $8,000 for new flats, resale with CPF Housing Grant, and HDB loans. New ECs $16,000 → $18,000 for land tenders closing on or after this date. HDB

          7. 28 Jul 2026

            15-month wait-out removed

            Private property owners and ex-owners can buy a resale flat without grants or an HDB loan right away, but must sell their private property within 6 months. The 30-month wait-out still applies for new flats, grants, HDB loans and ECs. HDB

          8. 8 May 2026

            10-year MOP for new EC sites

            ECs whose land tenders closed on or after 8 May 2026 can be sold on the open market only after 10 years from TOP. Other ECs keep the 5-year MOP. HDB

          9. 2025
          10. 28 Nov 2025

            One-off 2026 property tax rebate announced

            Owner-occupied HDB flats get 15% off their 2026 bill, and private homes 10% (capped at $500). 1- and 2-room HDB flats continue to pay no property tax. IRAS

          11. Jul 2025

            Family Care Scheme (Proximity)

            Replaces the Married Child and Senior Priority Schemes. Married and single children now get priority for new flats to live with or near their parents. HDB

          12. 4 Jul 2025

            Seller's Stamp Duty (SSD) back to 4 years

            For homes bought from this date, selling within 4 years attracts 16%, 12%, 8% or 4%. HDB owners aren't affected because of the MOP. MAS

          13. 1 Jan 2025

            Wider property tax bands for owner-occupiers

            The 0% band on owner-occupied homes widens from the first $8,000 to the first $12,000 of Annual Value, and the other bands shift up too. IRAS

          14. 2024
          15. Oct 2024

            Standard, Plus and Prime flats

            New flats are classified as Standard, Plus or Prime instead of mature or non-mature estates. Plus and Prime flats carry a 10-year MOP. First-timer singles can apply for 2-room Flexi flats in all locations. HDB

          16. 20 Aug 2024

            HDB loan limit cut to 75%; bigger grants

            HDB loans drop from 80% to 75% loan-to-value. The Enhanced CPF Housing Grant rises to up to $120,000 for families and $60,000 for singles. HDB

          17. 1 Jan 2024

            Property tax raised, step 2 of 2

            Homes not lived in by their owners are taxed at 12% to 36% (up from 10% to 20% before 2023). Owner-occupier rates on higher-value homes rise to a top rate of 32%. IRAS

          18. 2023
          19. 27 Apr 2023

            ABSD raised to current rates

            Citizens' 2nd home 20%, 3rd 30%; PRs' 2nd 30%, 3rd 35%; foreigners 60%; entities and trusts 65%. First homes stay at 0% for citizens and 5% for PRs. MAS

          20. 15 Feb 2023

            Buyer's Stamp Duty rates up to 6%

            Home values from $1.5 million to $3 million are charged 5%, and value above $3 million 6%. IRAS

          21. 14 Feb 2023

            Bigger CPF Housing Grants for resale flats

            Families: $50,000 → $80,000 for 4-room or smaller, $40,000 → $50,000 for 5-room or bigger. Singles: $25,000 → $40,000 and $20,000 → $25,000. HDB

          22. 1 Jan 2023

            Property tax raised, step 1 of 2

            More progressive rates for owner-occupied homes with an Annual Value above $30,000 and for all homes not lived in by their owners. MOF

          23. 2022
          24. 30 Sep 2022

            15-month wait-out; higher stress-test rates

            Private property owners must wait 15 months before buying a resale flat without grants (seniors 55+ moving to a 4-room or smaller flat are exempt). HDB loans drop from 85% to 80% loan-to-value. The bank stress-test rate rises from 3.5% to 4%, and HDB sets a 3% floor. MAS

          25. 2021
          26. 16 Dec 2021

            ABSD raised, TDSR and HDB loan limit tightened

            ABSD: citizens' 2nd home 17%, 3rd 25%; PRs' 2nd 25%, 3rd 30%; foreigners 30%; entities 35%. TDSR falls from 60% to 55%. HDB loans drop from 90% to 85% loan-to-value. MAS

          27. 27 Oct 2021

            Prime Location Public Housing (PLH) model

            New flats in prime central locations come with extra subsidies, a 10-year MOP, and subsidy recovery when sold. The first project, in Rochor, launches in the November 2021 BTO. HDB

          28. 2019
          29. 11 Sep 2019

            Enhanced CPF Housing Grant introduced; ceilings raised

            A single grant of up to $80,000 for first-timer families earning up to $9,000 replaces the Additional and Special CPF Housing Grants. The family income ceiling rises from $12,000 to $14,000, with singles set at $7,000 and new ECs at $16,000. HDB

          30. 10 May 2019

            CPF use and HDB loans linked to lease reaching age 95

            You can use CPF and HDB loans in full only if the remaining lease covers the youngest buyer to age 95. Otherwise the amounts are pro-rated. CPF

          31. 2018
          32. 6 Jul 2018

            ABSD raised, bank loan limits tightened

            ABSD: citizens' 2nd home 7% → 12%, 3rd 10% → 15%; PRs' 2nd and later homes 10% → 15%; foreigners 15% → 20%; entities 15% → 25%. Bank loan-to-value limits fall by 5 points, so a first home loan tops out at 75%. MAS

          33. 20 Feb 2018

            Buyer's Stamp Duty top rate raised to 4%

            The top rate on homes goes from 3% to 4%, charged on the value above $1 million. MOF

          34. 2017
          35. 11 Mar 2017

            Seller's Stamp Duty (SSD) eased

            The holding period drops from 4 to 3 years, and rates fall by 4 points to 12%, 8% and 4%. TDSR no longer applies to mortgage equity withdrawal loans of 50% loan-to-value or less. MAS

          36. 2016
          37. 1 Dec 2016

            Fresh Start Housing Scheme opens

            Second-timer families with children living in public rental flats can buy a 2-room Flexi or 3-room Standard flat on a shorter lease. These flats carry a 20-year MOP. HDB

          Sources used (all read on 17 Sep 2026). Eligibility rules come from HDB, CPF Board and URA. The loan calculator also uses IRAS (stamp duties, property tax) and MAS (bank loan limits). Legal and other fees aren't set by any agency, so they're your own estimates.
